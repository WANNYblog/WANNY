Working Mom or Full-Time? You Can't Get Away With Laundries!

There's always laundry every single day. That's why some wives struggle to organize it. Others send their laundry to a laundromat. But that costs them more. Is there any other tip to make things lighter? Yes. This article will help both working moms and full-time housewives.

The situation varies depending on the type of washer used. It's simpler if you have an automatic washer. But not everyone owns one. For example, with a manual machine, one still needs to transfer laundries to the spinner. And it takes more strength and time. Some still do hand washing because they don't have money to buy a machine yet.

1. For Hand Washing

If you have students and people working at home, uniforms must be the most important, next are underwear, towels, and socks.

The rest of the clothes like house clothes, leisure clothes, and ministry clothes can be scheduled:

  • Wednesday is for house clothes
  • Friday is for leisure clothes
  • Sunday is for Ministry clothes

Tip for heavy items: For bedsheets or curtains, you can just spend money on a "wash-only" service at laundromats. That way you won't have to carry heavy loads.

2. For Manual or Automatic Washers

It seems easier because it's less tiring, but it also consumes a lot of water and electricity so our bills get high. To make it lesser:

  • Sort out your clothes by color and type of fabric.
  • Separate light colors from dark.
  • Also, separate jeans and jackets.

Just like in the hand wash suggestion above, make sure school and work uniforms are prioritized and washed daily.

Then you can follow the schedule, or you can do laundry on weekends - Saturday and Sunday - so you only use the washer twice a week.

  • Saturday: House clothes and going-out clothes
  • Sunday: Church/Ministry clothes and heavy items like curtains and bedsheets

You can use wash only so you don't need to transfer to the dryer. Use the dryer only during the rainy season to save electricity.

I hope this article helps everyone. Situations may vary, so systems vary too. It depends on what's more convenient for you.
