7-Days ONE-DISH Budget Meal

P50 - P120 | Good for 4 People
For Busy Working Moms

And because of inflation rises, prices really give us a headache, so this article suggests cheaper versions that are still healthy and filling for the whole family.

Day 1 - Monday: Fried Rice Chicken Flavor P80.00

Rice from last night's dinner, 1 egg P6.00 + 1/4 kilo chicken breast P45.00 + carrots P15.00 + soy sauce. It only takes 10 minutes to cook. You already have rice and viand in one plate.

Day 2 - Tuesday: Sardines Omelette P45.00

1 sardines P25.00 + 2 eggs P12.00 + onion + tomato P10.00 for garnish on top of hot rice, you will be full.

Day 3 - Wednesday: Sauteed Monggo with Rice P60.00

Mung beans P20.00 + moringa leaves (just at your backyard) + 1 egg + 1 cup of rice mixed when cooking. A complete simple meal, doesn't need another dish.

Day 4 - Thursday: Potato Corned Beef with Rice P75.00

Small corned beef P35.00 + 2 potatoes P20.00 + garlic + rice P15.00 mixed altogether. Paella-like dish.

Day 5 - Friday: Ground Pork Meat with Rice P90.00

Ground pork 1/4 P60.00 + 2 eggs P12.00 + potato P15.00 mixed and fried together. Kids will surely love. Can also be for baon.

Day 6 - Saturday: Fried Sardines Rice + Egg P70.00

1 piece smoked fish P35.00 + rice + egg P6.00 + tomato. Just the smell will make you eat more.

Day 7 - Sunday: Adobo Rice P100.00

Adobo chicken sliced into small pieces + adobo sauce + rice + egg mixed in one pan. 2-in-1. Fewer dishes to wash.

Tipid Tip: Cook the rice and viand together in one pan. Less gas, less washing, less time.

You might wonder why there is no dinner suggestions? Because it is not healthy to eat in the evening a dish that was cooked in the morning. This article is for one cooking action in the morning that can also be used as baon for school and work. I will also make budget tips for supper in my next article.
